5 Budget Home Decor Items That Look $100 (Under $10)

Published: September 16, 2026 · 12 min read

The Test

We scrolled through 200+ Temu home decor items under $10. We bought the 30 most aesthetic ones, lived with them for 30 days, and picked the 5 that genuinely fooled our friends into asking "where did you get that?"

Our Top 5

1. Marble-Look Ceramic Vase (Set of 3) — $9.20

Rating: 9.5/10

Real marble vases cost $50-100. These look identical at a glance, weigh almost the same, and work beautifully with dried flowers or as standalone pieces.

Tip: Get the small-medium-large set. Display together for designer impact.

2. Brass-Look Candle Holders (Pair) — $6.80

Rating: 9/10

Solid-feeling metal, beautiful brushed brass finish. They photograph beautifully and look 10x the price on a dining table or mantel.

3. Velvet Throw Pillow Covers (Set of 2) — $4.50

Rating: 8.5/10

The velvet is genuinely soft (not the cheap shiny stuff). Hidden zipper looks clean. Multiple color options. Easily worth $20+ at HomeGoods.

4. Ceramic Planter with Stand — $7.95

Rating: 8.5/10

Matte ceramic with bamboo stand. Looks like West Elm. Our plant-loving team members bought extras for every room.

5. Scented Soy Candle in Glass Jar — $3.99

Rating: 8/10

Burns clean, 30+ hour burn time, throw scent is decent. Not as strong as luxury candles but the jar looks gorgeous even after burning.

What Didn't Make the Cut

  • "Marble" trays (looked cheap plastic up close)
  • "Brass" picture frames (finish wore off in a week)
  • "Velvet" ottomans (the velvet was rough)
  • "Linen" curtains (actually polyester, sheen was wrong)

Quick Decorating Rules for Budget Items

  • Group in threes: Always display small items in odd numbers
  • Vary heights: Tall + medium + small = designer look
  • Stick to a palette: 3 colors max per room
  • Mix textures: Glass + wood + metal + fabric
  • Hide the cheap: Put budget items in plain sight; hide the truly cheap

Final Thought

You don't need to spend hundreds to make your home look expensive. The trick is choosing the right items and styling them well. All 5 picks here are under $10 each.
