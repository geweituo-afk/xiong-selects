Temu Fashion: 8 Items Worth Buying (Tested 30+ Pieces)

Published: September 16, 2026 · 15 min read

Our Methodology

30+ clothing items ordered. Each washed 3+ times before judging. Worn by real people in real situations. We returned 22 of them. These 8 stayed in our closets.

The 8 Winners

1. Solid Color Cotton T-Shirts — $2.50-3.50

Rating: 9/10

Better than Uniqlo at 1/3 the price. Soft cotton, true to size, holds up after washing. Buy 5+ at a time.

2. Linen-Blend Wide-Leg Pants — $7.95

Rating: 9/10

Honestly looks like $60 pants. Comfortable, breathable, dress up or down. Just order 2 sizes up — they run small.

3. Oversized Knit Cardigans — $8.99

Rating: 8.5/10

Cozy fall/winter staple. Doesn't pill badly. The "oatmeal" color is gorgeous.

4. Basic Tank Tops (5-Pack) — $6.50

Rating: 8/10

For layering or under sheer tops. Surprisingly good quality for the price.

5. Pleated Midi Skirt — $5.99

Rating: 8/10

Work-appropriate, comfy, washes well. Looks much more expensive than it is.

6. Cotton Sleep Shorts (3-Pack) — $4.50

Rating: 8/10

Better than the premium brands we've tried. Comfy, soft, holds shape.

7. Knit Beanies — $2.99

Rating: 7.5/10

Solid basics. Not as thick as wool beanies but fine for casual wear.

8. Cotton Ankle Socks (10-Pack) — $3.99

Rating: 7/10

You wear them out anyway. At this price, you don't care.

The 22 Items We Returned

  • Dresses: Looked nothing like photos, fabric was scratchy
  • Denim: Cheap, stiff, weird fit
  • Blazers: Cheap polyester that looked obviously fake
  • "Silk" blouses: 100% polyester satin
  • Sweatshirts: Pilled after one wash

Tips for Buying Temu Fashion

  • Stick to basics — the harder the design, the worse the quality
  • Cotton > Polyester blends at this price
  • Always read the size chart (Asian sizing runs small)
  • Order multiple sizes if unsure (returns are easy)
  • Avoid anything labeled "silk" or "linen" under $15

Final Verdict

Temu basics are genuinely great. Stay away from anything "elevated" or "trendy" — that's where the quality drops. Stick to solids, cotton, and timeless cuts.
